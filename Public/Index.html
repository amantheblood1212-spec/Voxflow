FAQ

Straight answers about how VoxFlow works.

Is Alex a human?

No. Alex is an AI voice agent. If a prospect asks, she says so plainly.

Does VoxFlow call people who opted out?

No. A Do Not Contact request blocks that number for single calls, campaigns and scheduled follow-ups.

When does it make calls?

Only between 9am and 5pm, Monday to Friday, in the prospect's local time zone.

Is VoxFlow really free?

VoxFlow itself is free. Your phone and AI voice provider, phone numbers and any lead data services have their own costs.

What do I need to connect?

A telephony and AI voice provider account. You add its key and phone number ID on the Phone page. Keys stay on the server.

Is it legal to use?

VoxFlow includes opt-out and calling-hour controls, but you are responsible for following telemarketing and AI-voice rules, including consent requirements where they apply.
